Expected adult height

Mid-parental height · Tanner
Girls

(Father + Mother − 13) ÷ 2

Target range: result ± 8.5 cm

Example: father 172, mother 158 gives 158.5 cm, range 150 to 167 cm.

Boys

(Father + Mother + 13) ÷ 2

Target range: result ± 8.5 cm

The ± 8.5 cm range covers about the 3rd to 97th percentile. Measure parents where possible, as reported heights are often inaccurate.

4

If growth changes

Weight or appetite falling
  • Give the dose with or after food
  • Early breakfast and a late evening meal
  • Dietitian for high-calorie, nutritious foods
  • Consider a planned break or a change of medication

NICE 1.8.6

Height falling below expected
  • Check height speed against parents' heights and puberty
  • Rule out other causes: thyroid, coeliac disease, Turner syndrome
  • Review the dose and consider a break over school holidays
  • Refer to paediatric endocrinology

NICE 1.8.7

What we tell the family

Evidence Card · October 2026

What the Evidence Says

Four clinical questions, each with a verdict and the studies behind it.

Dr. Mohammed AlOtaibi · Child and Adolescent Psychiatry

Question 1Does it slow growth?Yes, early and modestly
Question 2Does it reduce adult height?Usually little or none
Question 3Who is most at risk?Years of daily use from early childhood
Question 4Can we reverse it?Weight yes, height no
1

Does it slow growth?

Verdict · Yes, early and modestly
Meta-analysis

Height and weight percentiles fall on methylphenidate. Weight falls mostly in the first 12 months, height in the first 24 to 30 months. Dose, formulation and age did not change the effect.Carucci 2021 · 18 studies · 4,868 children

Meta-analysis

The newest pooled analysis confirms small reductions in height, weight and BMI percentiles.Cao 2026 · 33 studies

Cohort

ADHD itself is linked to temporary height deficits in early adolescence, unrelated to medication, that normalise later.Spencer 1996 · 124 ADHD vs 109 controls

2

Does it reduce adult height?

Verdict · Usually little or none
Controlled cohort

No difference in height growth over two years between treated and untreated ADHD. Puberty was not delayed.ADDUCE 2023 and 2024 · 1,410 children, 27 European centres

Birth cohort

No difference in adult height or in the size of the growth spurt.Harstad 2014 · 340 ADHD vs 680 controls

National cohort

Adult height 167.8 vs 167.9 cm, children with and without ADHD.Song 2026, Korea · 34,850 young people

Long-term trial

Regular users were 2.6 cm shorter as adults than those with negligible use.MTA, Swanson 2017 · 515 children followed to age 25

3

Who is most at risk?

Verdict · Long, early, high exposure
Long-term trial

The 53 children who took stimulants consistently for 16 years were 4.1 cm shorter as adults than negligible users.MTA, Greenhill 2020

Chart review

Children started younger and taken to high doses lost more height percentile than those on usual doses.Ross 2020 · 52 high dose vs 118 usual dose

Growth data

Girls usually gain about 6.6 cm after their first period. Before it, more growth is at stake.Post-menarcheal cohort, PubMed 38295778

4

Can we reverse it?

Verdict · Weight yes, height no
Randomised trial

Children whose BMI fell on stimulants were given drug holidays, a daily 150 kcal supplement, or monthly monitoring. All improved weight gain. None improved height growth.Waxmonsky 2020 · 230 children aged 5 to 12

Open-label

On atomoxetine, a height shortfall peaked at 18 months and returned to expected by 24 months.Spencer 2007 · 1,312 children

Long-term trial

Adults treated consistently in the MTA weighed more than their classmates. Watch weight in both directions.MTA, Greenhill 2020

5

Bottom line for the clinic

ExpectA small early slowing in the first two to three years.
ReassureMost children reach close to their expected adult height.
Act earlyProtect weight from the start, and measure height every six months.
